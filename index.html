The Origin

Inspired by the wild.
Defined by the cast.

Holy Buck began with a simple idea: the forms nature creates deserve to be seen as sculpture.

Antlers are temporary by nature. They're grown, carried, and shed. Casting transforms that fleeting form into something with weight, presence, and permanence.

Each work begins with an original form, is recreated as a physical pattern, cast in metal, and finished by hand. The casting is not hidden behind the art. It is part of its character.
